BUSINESS

Four businesses connecting Japan and India

01

Japanese Language Education

In partnership with universities across India, we provide Japanese language education to ambitious young Indians using a curriculum aligned with the JLPT (Japanese Language Proficiency Test). We support the entire journey — from education to recruitment and post-employment retention.

Our partner universities include nursing and IT institutions, allowing us to supply talent for a wide range of industries beyond IT.

Track Record: In partnership with NISSO Co., Ltd. (listed on the Prime Market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange), we provide end-to-end services from Japanese language education to recruitment of Indian talent.

02

Recruitment

Drawing on a database of over 10,000 Indian IT engineers, we introduce the right talent for your company primarily through our full-time recruitment service, IT Jobs Japan (IJJ). From hiring experienced engineers ready to contribute immediately, to developing new graduates from top universities, we offer flexible options to fit your needs.

Beyond IT talent, we also place candidates under the "Specified Skilled Worker" visa category, including nursing professionals. We connect people between Japan and India across a wide range of fields — IT, caregiving, healthcare, and more.

03

Offshore Development

Leveraging our network of local entities in India, we offer offshore development — both dedicated lab-type teams and contract-based projects — backed by more than 10,000 Indian engineers. We cover everything from web and app development to cutting-edge technologies such as blockchain and AI.

For clients concerned about communicating in English, our bilingual "Bridge SEs" — well versed in Japanese business practices — join your team to manage progress and deadlines, giving you peace of mind.

04

India Market Entry Support

We support Japanese companies entering the Indian market as their local partner. We are currently leading a marketing project in India for the "Breast Care Glove," a breast self-examination aid developed by Japan's ICST Corporation.

Why This Matters to Us: India ranks third in the world for breast cancer incidence (approximately 190,000 cases per year), yet has the highest number of deaths from the disease worldwide — approximately 98,000 per year (GLOBOCAN 2022). This disproportionately high mortality rate is largely attributed to delayed detection. Through this technology, which has over 15 years of use and roughly 1.7 million units sold in Japan, we hope to help more women achieve early detection and treatment. That is the belief driving this project.
